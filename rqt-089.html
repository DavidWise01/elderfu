RQT-089 · Hodge Caliper / Period Pairings

In X=CP¹×CP¹ let a,b be divisor classes with a²=b²=0 and ∫X a∪b=1. For alpha=p a+q b, measurement along the A-curve (P¹×point, class b) is p, and along B-curve (point×P¹, class a) is q. The implementation labels probes by their paired dual classes to avoid orientation ambiguity.
   
Exact algebraic cycles p·(point×P¹) + q·(P¹×point) yield alpha (up to stated basis choices). This is a validated elementary Hodge example, not a new solution to the conjecture. The nested syntax is an encoding, not itself an algebraic theorem.
