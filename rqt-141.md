In the integer basis of the norm kernel, the difference map K=(Z⁻¹XZ−I)[1:,1:] is an invertible 21×21 integer matrix. Its Smith factors are 1 repeated seven times and 2 repeated fourteen times. Hence H¹(C4,L)=Z²¹/KZ²¹ is elementary two-torsion of dimension 14.

Take the eight existing obstruction generators from fixed glue representatives (RQT-139/140), represented by their rational 21-vectors K⁻¹ d modulo Z²¹. Encode each entry modulo 1 as one F2 bit after multiplying by two. The known vectors have F2 rank 8.

For the 21 unit cocycles d=e_j, compute K⁻¹ e_j mod Z²¹. The full candidate list spans F2 rank 14. The eight plus six have rank 14, and all 64 combinations of the six are separately checked for independence modulo the eight-dimensional image. Twice each supplementary obstruction vanishes modulo integers; each unit cocycle lies in the four-tick norm kernel.
