|||| RQT-073 · RECOIL / FIELD MOMENTUM

Three-component impulse ledger
A specified momentum impulse is received by the particle; a selected fraction is assigned to the electromagnetic field, and the rest to the boundary. This enforces recoil conservation by construction; it does not derive an electromagnetic stress.
     
Electromagnetic reference
In SI vacuum electrodynamics the electromagnetic momentum density is g=ε₀(E×B)=S/c². Maxwell stress and boundary flux are necessary for a dynamical derivation of field impulse. The ledger does NOT calculate E or B fields and does not demonstrate that the selected impulse fractions are physically realizable. The four interfaces and four force-channel names remain author-defined routing labels.
