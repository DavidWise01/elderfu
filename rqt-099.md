## Strict statement
A rational endomorphism of a weight-two rational Hodge structure defines a Hodge tensor of bidegree (2,2) on the corresponding self-product when it respects the Hodge decomposition (with the usual polarization identification). Consider a **formal 2D K3-type rational Hodge structure**, with H^(2,0) generated by omega=e1+i e2. For M=[[a,b],[c,d]], the preservation condition M(omega)=lambda omega reduces exactly to **a=d and c=-b**. 

Test I = [[1,0],[0,1]]: Hodge PASS, known diagonal-type correspondence in a genuine polarized K3 setting. J=[[0,-1],[1,0]]: Hodge PASS **on this abstract plane**, but we have not provided an algebraic K3 surface, its embedding/periods, or a corresponding algebraic cycle. STOP certificate search. Reflection [[1,0],[0,-1]]: Hodge FAIL; cannot enter the conjecture as a Hodge-class candidate in this model. Therefore no argument about its algebraicity.

These are exact linear-algebra calculations, **not** a general K3 Hodge conjecture test. In particular, known results on rational Hodge isometries (Buskin) can apply to genuine K3 surfaces; we do not label J a new open case. Reports of 2026 general K3-product proof were **withdrawn in October 2026** due to a proof gap; do not use them as a certificate.

The literal first negative-control failure is *Hodge type* (reflection). For arbitrary K3 primitive tensors, a general constructive algebraic-cycle algorithm is not furnished. The conjecture remains open. Three witnesses: A rational matrix, B Hodge-period test, C explicit cycle/realization. No quorum. Preserve RQT-068 freeze.
