## Construction

For the order-four geometric automorphism X on the 22-dimensional integral Fermat quartic cohomology lattice M, let N=I+X+X²+X³. Reuse the **primitive integer norm-kernel basis** V ∈ Z^{22×21} and the 21×22 matrix C representing (X-I)M within ker_Z(N) from RQT-142. Set F=columnHNF(C); |det F|=4096. The group H¹(C4,M) is represented exactly by rational residue vectors F⁻¹d mod Z²¹, d∈Z²¹.

Choose the six standard basis cocycles e_j at zero-based norm-kernel positions **0,1,9,10,15,19**. Each lies in ker_Z(N), has exact order four modulo (X-I)M, and has an integral 22-dimensional representative V e_j. Exhaustively enumerating all 4⁶ coefficient tuples produces **4096 distinct residues**, proving these six cocycles generate the entire ambient group (Z/4)^6.

Double each generator and compare with the six surviving order-two primitive-sector cocycles identified in RQT-141 at norm-kernel coordinates 0,2,3,4,5,6. The explicit change-of-basis matrix over F₂ has determinant 1 modulo 2. Hence the doubles of the six new generators span **exactly** the previously established ambient two-torsion subgroup (Z/2)^6, not merely a subgroup of it.

This is a constructive closure of RQT-142's non-split extension, **not** an identification of canonical geometric generators, nor a proof that the six cohomological difference classes are algebraic cycles.
