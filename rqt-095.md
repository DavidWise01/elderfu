## Mathematical gate
For a smooth complex projective X, dimension d and ample class L, Hard Lefschetz proves `L^(d-k): H^k(X,Q) -> H^(2d-k)(X,Q)` is a vector-space isomorphism for k<=d. The inverse exists **as a linear map**. That does *not by itself* give an **algebraic correspondence** representing it. The universal correspondence assertion is the Lefschetz standard conjecture (B), open in general; it implies algebraicity of the Künneth projectors (C). These are necessary-but-not-sufficient progress markers toward the full Hodge conjecture, and none is a counterexample.

## Exact executable positive control
`rqt-095.py`: CP3, H*(CP3,Q)=Q[h]/(h^4). L^3 sends 1->h³; L sends h->h². Both are one-dimensional exact isomorphisms and known to have algebraic-correspondence realizations on projective space. This only validates the finite-dimensional control calculation.
