|||| RQT-068 · Stress / Expansion Work

Single expanding bubble · electrum as one optional magnetic component. Test the mismatch between isotropic effective pressure and directional Maxwell stress.

Radius multiplier: 2.00×

Boundary interpretation
B=B₀(R₀/R)² is imposed. U=B²V/(2μ₀), V=4πR³/3. The isotropic energy-derived pressure is p_eff=−dU/dV=u_B/3. Treating magnetic energy density u_B as a uniform spherical pressure would predict 3× too much work. Actual Maxwell stress depends on surface normal: T_ij=(B_i B_j−½δ_ij B²)/μ₀. For a uniform axial interior B, normal traction changes with latitude and is not an outward uniform pressure. External fields, surface currents, source work, and field-line geometry are not solved. This is NOT a self-contained magnetically inflated bubble.
