## Construction

Fermat quartic S in P³ has 48 lines in three matching families of two coordinate pairs, each described by `x_a + zeta^u x_b=0, x_c+zeta^v x_d=0` with u,v in {1,3,5,7}, zeta a primitive 8th root of unity. Distinct projective lines meet if and only if the joint system of four linear equations has rank less than four. The audit uses F17 where 2 is a primitive eighth root, computes line incidence there, and lifts the resulting 0/1 meeting pattern into the *integral* Gram matrix with self-intersections -2 by adjunction. The chosen specialization should not identify lines or create accidental extra intersections: for this explicit roots-of-unity configuration the incidence equalities are combinatorial identities in eighth-root exponents, and the resulting matrix agrees with published geometric lattice results.

The exact matrix has 336 unordered intersecting pairs, rank 20, and a 20-line principal submatrix with determinant -64. That equals the known Néron–Severi discriminant (negative sign from signature (1,19)). Since published results establish that the 48 line classes generate NS(S), the calculation gives a genuine Gram representation for NS(S), not just an illustrative Gram matrix.

The three g1,g2,g3 of RQT-110 send every one of the 48 line equations to another; their permutations preserve **all** pairings. The nested projector images on NS have ranks 12,8,6, and adding the known fixed T(S) rank 2 yields the full cohomology ranks 14,10,8.
