## Mathematical construction
Choose a smooth quartic K3 surface S⊂P³, with hyperplane class h∈H²(S,Q) satisfying ∫_S h²=4. Define on S×S the codimension-two rational algebraic correspondence

`P = [Δ_S] - (1/4) pr_1^*h · pr_2^*h.`

Its action on degree-two cohomology is `P_*(x)=x-(∫_S x∪h / 4) h`. Hence P_*(h)=0, P_* fixes primitive v with ∫vh=0, and P_*²=P_*.

The Python file performs exact rational checks on a formal polarization/primitive two-component subspace. It does NOT compute an entire K3 period lattice, produce new algebraic classes, or verify the general Hodge conjecture.
