## Exact results

Let the ambient even unimodular rank-22 Fermat K3 marking have order-four action X, with primitive spectral sum L from RQT-136 and glue quotient G=H²(S,Z)/L of order 512. RQT-137 gives generators g0 (order four), g1–g7 (order two). The RQT-138 transition changes only b3 by a modulo two. Thus a class is fixed exactly when a is even, producing the rank-eight elementary abelian group G^X ≅ (Z/2)^8. A concrete generating set is 2g0,g1,...,g7; all 256 combinations are enumerated and distinct by exact fractional coordinates Z^(-1)e mod Z^22.

All pairings within the full glue subgroup are integral, and all its norms even, so the induced discriminant bilinear and quadratic forms vanish on the fixed subgroup. This concerns the discriminant form of the **primitive spectral sum**, not a claim that the ambient intersection form itself is zero.

The key distinction is between **fixed cosets** and **fixed vectors**. The full integer kernel ker_Z(X-I) is primitive rank one, generated by ±h (the hyperplane class), with h²=4. Since this kernel is contained in the primitive sum L, every pointwise X-fixed integral representative maps to the **zero** coset in G. Therefore exactly one of the 256 fixed cosets, the zero class, admits an individually pointwise fixed integral lift. The other **255 fixed cosets** do not. This does not prevent X from permuting or shifting integral representatives of the fixed cosets by elements of L.
