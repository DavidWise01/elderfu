RQT-090 · Blind Hodge Caliper

Known test space: X = CP¹ × CP¹; rational divisor-class basis a,b with a²=b²=0, ∫a∪b=1. Waldo sees only two intersection measurements, not the hidden coefficients. All calculations use reduced exact rational fractions.
  
The experiment is a blind recovery of coefficients from a nondegenerate known pairing, not discovery of a new algebraic cycle on an arbitrary variety. In CP¹×CP¹, every rational degree-2 Hodge class is an algebraic-cycle class. General Hodge conjecture remains open. No physical gravitational interpretation is inferred.
