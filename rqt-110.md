## Geometric construction

These three matrices preserve the smooth Fermat quartic; they all have determinant +1, order 2 in PGL4, and commute **projectively**, although the chosen matrix lifts may anticommute. Thus they generate G=(Z/2)^3, order 8, consisting of symplectic projective automorphisms, with algebraic graph correspondences Γ_g ⊂ S×S. Every nonidentity element is a symplectic involution.

By Nikulin's fixed-point theorem, each nonidentity involution has 8 isolated fixed points and therefore trace 6 on H²(S,Q), as χ(Fix)=2+tr(H²). With identity trace 22 and character orthogonality, the simultaneous character decomposition of H² consists of one trivial character of multiplicity 8 and seven nontrivial characters each of multiplicity 2: 8+7×2=22.

The sequential invariant spaces of g1, then g2, then g3 have ranks 14,10,8, respectively. The discarded branches have ranks 8,4,2. Each P_g±=(Δ±Γ_g)/2 is a rational algebraic correspondence; reversing with all branches recovers the original cohomology vector. Unlike earlier proxy stages, these are genuine algebraic maps, although the Python cohomology representation is a character model based on the known fixed-point theorem, not an independently computed H² period basis.
