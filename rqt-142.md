## Exact ambient calculation

Let M=H²(S,Z) be the rank-22 **integral geometric** K3 lattice from RQT-133 and X its order-four automorphism. Set N=I+X+X²+X³ and D=X−I. The norm has rank one, while ker_Z N is a primitive rank-21 integral sublattice. A unimodular integer column operation gives a certified 22×21 basis V0 of ker_Z N. In this basis, the image D M is a rank-21 integral sublattice represented by C, an integer 21×22 matrix.

The **exact Smith invariant factors of C** are 1 repeated fifteen times and 4 repeated six times. Therefore

H¹(C4,M) = ker_Z N / D M ≅ (Z/4)^6,

a group of **4^6 = 4096** classes. This is not the (Z/2)^6 group one might incorrectly obtain by canceling torsion dimensions without considering extensions.

RQT-141 established H¹(C4,L)≅(Z/2)^14 for the index-512 primitive spectral sum L, and an eight-dimensional subgroup of fixed-glue differences. Those eight generators are actual coboundaries in M, as explicitly checked against the integral ambient image C.

Since (Z/4)^6 has exactly 64 elements killed by two, the image is its entire two-torsion subgroup, 2H¹(C4,M)≅(Z/2)^6.

Consequently the kernel of H¹(C4,L)→H¹(C4,M) is exactly the eight-dimensional fixed-glue obstruction subgroup, and the cokernel is (Z/2)^6. The resulting ambient extension is non-split: the full ambient group contains order-four elements.
