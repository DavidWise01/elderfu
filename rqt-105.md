## Mathematical object

S is the Fermat quartic x^4+y^4+z^4+w^4=0 in P^3. The symplectic involution ι:[x:y:z:w]↦[x:y:−z:−w] has eight isolated fixed points on S. The singular quotient S/<ι> has explicit weighted-projective equations with coordinates (x,y,A=z²,B=w²,C=zw): x⁴+y⁴+A²+B²=0; C²=AB. Its minimal resolution Y is the K3 surface Km(E_i×E_i). Thus Y is projective K3, and S dashrightarrow Y is a rational map of generic degree 2. The closure of its graph in S×Y is an *explicitly defined algebraic correspondence*.

A published integral-lattice computation identifies T(S)=diag(8,8), T(Y)=diag(4,4), and T(Y)(2)=T(S). Discriminants 64 and 16 differ: a complex K3 isomorphism would preserve integral transcendental lattices, hence **S and Y are non-isomorphic**. The induced Hodge map has intersection-form multiplier 2, so we must not label it an isometry. This comparison goes beyond the isomorphic coordinates of RQT-104.
