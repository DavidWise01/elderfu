## Test object
Let X be an arbitrary smooth projective variety over C with complex dimension d. Let Y = X × X and Δ_X ⊂ Y be its algebraic diagonal, whose rational class is in H^{2d}(Y,Q). Its Künneth components in H^a(X,Q)⊗H^b(X,Q) with a+b=2d are rational Hodge classes. The total diagonal has an algebraic cycle witness; individual Künneth components need not have a known algebraic cycle witness in general. This is an example discussed in Pierre Deligne's official Clay Hodge problem description. Importantly, an explicit X, component, and proof of nonalgebraicity were **not** supplied; absence of a witness is NOT proof of a counterexample.
