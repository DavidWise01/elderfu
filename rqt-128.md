## Reproducible finite search

The Fermat quartic x⁴+y⁴+z⁴+w⁴=0 has a monomial group of projective coordinate automorphisms of order 4³·4! = 1536. Enumerate all permutations σ∈S4 and all projective coordinate phase choices (i^a,i^b,i^c,1). Transform the defining linear equations for each of the 48 Fermat lines over F17, using an order-eight root of unity 2, and identify their images. Verify the resulting 48-element permutations preserve the **actual integral line-intersection matrix G**.

Let B be the selected 20-line Gram matrix of determinant −64. The unique coordinates of the 48 line classes in the chosen 20-line basis are C=B⁻¹G[piv,:], all integral. For each symmetry, its integral 20×20 action is W=C[:,mapped(piv)]. Check every entry is integral, WᵀBW=B, det(W)=±1.

Reduce W acting on both fractional discriminant generators U from RQT-114 modulo Z²⁰. Exact arithmetic identifies precisely four discriminant actions:
- identity [[1,0],[0,1]]: 384 symmetries;
- negative identity [[7,0],[0,7]]: 384;
- quarter-turn [[0,1],[7,0]]: 384;
- inverse quarter-turn [[0,7],[1,0]]: 384.

The target transition Q=[[7,4],[4,7]] (mod 8) from RQT-124 occurs **zero** times. Thus no element of this 1536-element coordinate automorphism subgroup realizes Q. This exhausts this finite subgroup, **not** the full integral orthogonal group O(NS), nor the full set of possible isometries that do not preserve the ample cone. A lattice isometry need not arise from any automorphism of S.
