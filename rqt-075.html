|||| RQT-075 · FIELD EXCITATION / DISPERSION

One expanding bubble · two field-excitation approximations
Evolution time  × 10⁻¹⁸ s 
Scientific scope
The test uses a 1D nonrelativistic free Gaussian Schrödinger wave packet as a surrogate for a localized electron-field excitation. Free width σ(t)=σ₀√(1+(ℏt/(2mσ₀²))²). A separate ideal harmonic oscillator ground state is stationary at width σ₀ if its potential frequency is chosen accordingly; that field/potential is externally imposed, NOT generated by the bubble. Probability inside |x|≤R is erf(R/(√2σ)). Here R(t)=R₀+ut is prescribed. The four interfaces are structural labels only; no electrum constitutive law, relativistic quantum field theory or actual self-consistent boundary potential is solved. Probability leakage does not mean charge or matter disappeared.
