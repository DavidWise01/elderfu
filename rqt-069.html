Magnetic traction, latitude by latitude

B(R)=B₀(R₀/R)² is an assumed flux law. The displayed magnetic stress is the interior uniform-field contribution only; not an exterior-interior stress jump.

Surface equilibrium and missing mechanisms
For an internal uniform axial field, the Maxwell normal traction is tₙ(θ)=u_B(2cos²θ−1), u_B=B²/(2μ₀). Its spherical area-average is −u_B/3 (with this traction convention). Because its pole and equator signs differ, a spatially constant pressure correction cannot cancel it everywhere. Surface tension 2γ/R and a uniform pressure jump add only angle-independent terms; a field outside, anisotropic shell stress, surface currents, or deformation would be needed to solve the full force balance. This app does not compute a self-consistent equilibrium.
