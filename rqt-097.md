Let S=P¹×P¹, a²=b²=0, ∫ab=1, L=a+b. A codimension-1 cycle on S×S is `Z=pr1^*L+pr2^*L`. With standard correspondence action Z_*(x)=(pr2)_*(pr1^*x∪[Z]), Z_*(1)=0, Z_*(a)=Z_*(b)=1, Z_*(ab)=L. This is the lowering Λ for this surface: [Λ,L]=2 on H⁰, 0 on H², -2 on H⁴ (using convention ΛL-LΛ). The H⁰→H⁴ inverse of L² is (1/2)[S×S]. Identity on H² is induced by the algebraic diagonal Δ_S (also acts as identity in all degrees). These are exact known constructions; they do not establish universal Lefschetz standard conjecture B or Hodge conjecture. No quorum; frozen RQT-068 unchanged.
