## Exact sequence

Let M=H²(S,Z), for the actual 22-class Fermat quartic marking from RQT-122/133, with order-four geometric automorphism X and norm N=I+X+X²+X³. Let h be the primitive hyperplane class with chosen coordinate vector (1,1,1,1,0,...,0). Exact matrix computations give N(M)=Z*h, rank(N)=1, rank_Z ker(N)=21, Xh=h, h²=4, and N(e0)=h.

A primitive basis K (22×21) for ker_Z N is constructed with unimodular Euclidean column operations. The integral 22×22 matrix P=[e0|K] has det(P)=+1, proving M=Z*e0 ⊕ ker_Z N as abelian groups and certifying an integral section s(h)=e0 of N.

## Obstruction to equivariant section

The integral pointwise-fixed lattice is M^X=Z*h, as follows from rank(X-I)=21 and primitivity of h. Any X-equivariant section would require an X-fixed integer preimage of h, which would be m*h with N(m*h)=4m*h. No integer m satisfies 4m=1. Thus **no X-equivariant integral section exists**.

The section defect is delta=(X-I)e0=e4-e0∈ker_Z(N). It represents an element of H¹(C4,ker N)=ker(N)/(X-I)ker(N) of **exact order four**. Indeed, 4e0-h∈ker(N) and (X-I)(4e0-h)=4delta. Conversely, if n*delta=(X-I)k for some k∈ker N, then n*e0−k is X-fixed and equals m*h; applying N gives n*h=4m*h, forcing 4|n. Hence neither one nor two times delta can be a coboundary in ker(N).

**STOP precisely at equivariance:** the integral norm sequence is split, and the order-four obstruction proves there is no equivariant split. This does not deny the projective automorphism or the return X⁴=I.
