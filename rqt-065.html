|||| RQT-065 · Conserved Magnetic Flux

One expanding bubble. Electrum is an optional magnetic component; B decreases as R⁻² when flux is prescribed constant.

Field law 

Model boundary
For circular equatorial area A=πR², imposed flux Φ=B(R)A=πR₀²B₀. A changing magnetic field normally entails an induced electric field under Faraday's law. This controlled experiment omits that induced field, so it is NOT a self-consistent Maxwell solution. Magnetic energy is not accounted for as a conserved total-system ledger. Motion is a softened Coulomb+Boris toy model.
