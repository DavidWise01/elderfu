## Exact test
Define smooth Fermat quartic K3 S⊂P³ by x⁴+y⁴+z⁴+w⁴=0. Its smoothness follows because the four homogeneous partial derivatives vanish simultaneously only at the forbidden all-zero coordinates. The projective linear transformation g:[x:y:z:w]→[ix:y:z:w] preserves this equation, has order four, and graph Γ_g⊂S×S is an algebraic correspondence. On the one-dimensional holomorphic H^{2,0} line, the standard residue 2-form scales by i, since g acts on ambient 4-form by determinant i and preserves F. On H^{0,2}, the eigenvalue is −i.

**What passed:** exact SymPy check for polynomial invariance, A⁴=I, det A=i. Algebraicity of the graph follows immediately from the explicit projective automorphism. These give a rational Hodge correspondence, though expressing the period eigenvalue uses complex extension of the rational map and its chosen holomorphic form.

**FAIL-STOP:** We did NOT construct an arbitrary rational (2,2) cycle on S×S, determine a complete Hodge tensor basis, or prove the Hodge conjecture. A graph correspondence is a specific positive witness only; not a universal spanning theorem.
