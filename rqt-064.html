|||| RQT-064 · Electrum Component / Magnetic Coupling

One expanding bubble; proton fixed as a heavy-center approximation; electron experiences Coulomb force plus an optional uniform perpendicular magnetic field.

Physics and limits
Force: F = F_Coulomb + q(v × B). Uniform B points out of the 2D drawing. Magnetic acceleration is advanced by Boris rotation, which conserves speed for a magnetic-only push. Coulomb acceleration uses symmetric half-kicks; a finite step and softening cause numerical energy drift. The prescribed expanding wall can do work. Electrum is the author's name for this optional component, not a claim that an electrum metal alloy is generating the field.
