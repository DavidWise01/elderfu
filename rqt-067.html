|||| RQT-067 · Energy Closure

One growing bubble. Model-specific electrum magnetic component. Particle and magnetic field energies enter one ledger; the unmodeled external work is recorded as a residual.

Energy ledger: prescribed-field closure, not measured conservation
Assume a uniform field inside a sphere: U_B = B²(4πR³/3)/(2μ₀). For flux-conserving B∝R⁻², U_B∝R⁻¹. The external work required is defined here as ΔE_particle + ΔU_B. Without calculating an independently specified wall/source stress-energy, a zero accounting residual is tautological, not a proof of energy conservation. The finite-size B cutoff and E fields are not a self-consistent Maxwell solution.
Inside the spatially uniform axial field region, B(t)=B₀[R₀/R(t)]² and dB/dt=−2B(R)u/R. By Faraday's law Eφ(r,t)=−(r/2)dB/dt. In Cartesian coordinates Ex=(y/2)dB/dt and Ey=−(x/2)dB/dt. This is a local idealized interior field, not a global finite-bubble Maxwell solution. E and B are externally prescribed; field energy, sources, radiation and boundary continuity are not solved.
Numerical update: Coulomb + induced electric half-kicks, Boris magnetic rotation, position drift, second half-kick. Particle mechanical energy change should approximately equal ∫q E·v dt (the Coulomb potential is already included). Errors indicate discretization, not new physics.
