## Exact cohomology sequence

Since the fixed integral lattice M^X is Z*h, H⁰(C4,K)=0 and H⁰(C4,M)=Z*h. The induced norm from fixed M to Z*h sends m h to 4m h. Thus the initial long exact sequence includes

The connecting map takes h to the class of delta=(X−I)e0=e4−e0. With P=[e0|K_basis] unimodular and Y the 21×21 restriction of X to K, the difference map D=Y−I has exact Smith diagonal 1 repeated 14 times and 4 repeated 7 times, hence H¹(C4,K)=Z²¹/DZ²¹≅(Z/4)^7. The four-tick norm on K vanishes.

Directly, 4delta=(X−I)(4e0−h), with 4e0−h∈K. Adjoining delta to the columns of D changes the Smith diagonal to 1 repeated 15 times and 4 repeated 6 times. Therefore quotienting H¹(C4,K) by im(connecting)≅Z/4 produces H¹(C4,M)≅(Z/4)^6, matching RQT-142.
