## Concrete experiment

The Fermat quartic Néron–Severi group NS has rank 20 and is generated by the 48 lines. From RQT-114, use its actual 20-line intersection matrix B (det −64) and hyperplane class h equal to a sum of four coplanar lines. For a selected subset I of 3 or 5 line indices, define f = 2h − Σ_{i∈I} L_i. Verify exactly f²=0 in the full 48-line Gram matrix. Find a specific Fermat line M with f·M=1, set e=f+M, and calculate the integral coordinates of f and e in the selected 20-line basis. Because M²=−2, e²=0 and f·e=1; the Gram on (f,e) is the integral hyperbolic plane U.

For each of 20 explicit cases (8 triples and 12 quintuples; exact indices in [rqt-127.py](./rqt-127.py)), complete f,e to an integral unimodular basis and extract the orthogonal 18-dimensional negative-definite complement N18 of determinant 64. An approximate Cholesky factor is used **only** to select a unimodular LLL reduction, which is verified by exact integer determinant. Root enumeration then uses exact rational LDL decomposition and exhaustive conservative integer intervals, not floating-point thresholds.

Result: **all 20 complements have exactly 28 norm−2 roots.** The benchmark E8(−1)² has 240+240=480 norm−2 roots, so none of the 20 candidate complements has the requested orthogonal E8(−1)² factor. The independent RQT-126 complement also had 28 roots.

## Strict scope and STOP

These are **20 selected primitive U embeddings**, not an exhaustive search over all embeddings or all isotropic classes of the indefinite NS lattice. Identical root counts do not prove they are pairwise isometric. Nor does failure for these candidates contradict the previously proposed abstract standard-lattice presentation. **STOP** before a claim that no primitive U embedding with E8(−1)² exists; the next attempt should search a distinct isotropic orbit or construct the integral discriminant-form isometry directly in the 20-line coordinates.

Three non-voting witnesses A: divisor intersections, B: complete root enumeration, C: searched-set scope. Preserve `{{ i + h }}`, `{[(i)]}`, .dlw, ENDER, cubit, append-only boundary records, and immutable RQT-068. General Hodge conjecture remains open.
