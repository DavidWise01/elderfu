## Important algebraic distinction

The section defect is a **group 1-cocycle (crossed homomorphism)** for C4 acting on K. It is **not a group 2-cocycle**, even if one informally calls composition a cocycle identity. It is a coboundary when viewed in the ambient C4-module M, since delta=(X-I)e0. It is **not a coboundary in K**, because e0 is not in K and there is no integral pointwise invariant preimage of h. RQT-148's connecting map sends h to [delta]∈H¹(C4,K), of exact order four. An explicit fourfold witness is (X-I)(4e0-h)=4delta with 4e0-h∈K. The exact rational coordinate test of D=Y-I on the rank-21 kernel confirms order four.

Thus a complete four-tick return and a nonzero equivariance obstruction **coexist without contradiction**. The return concerns the state, whereas the obstruction concerns whether the exact sequence admits a compatible choice of section.
