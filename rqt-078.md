## Executed results

Independent Python exact-rational checks covered n=2,4,6,8 for each of two known projective varieties. The B/T wrappers preserve the payload by identity and therefore preserve the encoded ring relationships and named algebraic basis classes. 8 wrapper/variety scenarios pass. Negative control: under h -> h-2 in Q[h]/(h³), (h-2)³ = -8+12h-6h² != 0, so this is not a ring automorphism.

This confirms lossless serialization/transport on already-understood examples, **not** a new Hodge result. Algebraic-cycle existence in unknown cases is not deduced by wrapping. The general conjecture remains open.
