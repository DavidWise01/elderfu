## Mathematical test

RQT-114 constructed an even unimodular Gram matrix H with 20 integral line-class generators and two glued generators g1=u+e1/8, g2=v+e2/8, where T=diag(8,8). In that glued integral basis define t1=8g1−8u and t2=8g2−8v. Direct exact symbolic matrix multiplication confirms t1²=t2²=8 and t1·t2=0. Set omega=t1+i t2, so omega²=0 and omega·conjugate(omega)=16>0. Every chosen line-class generator has zero pairing with omega. The formal rational orthogonal quarter-turn J(t1)=-t2, J(t2)=t1 satisfies J(omega)=i omega.

These are genuine necessary K3 period conditions, and the period line is a legitimate *abstract polarized Hodge structure* compatible with the lattice. In this rank-two transcendental positive plane it has the CM-type orientation expected for the Fermat quartic. However the calculation does not evaluate analytic periods of the Fermat quartic holomorphic 2-form on actual integral 2-cycles, prove an identified marking, nor derive the projective automorphism's action on all 22 glued generators. That is the **first proof stop**. The general Hodge conjecture remains open.
