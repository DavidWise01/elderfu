## Exact arithmetic
The diagonal coordinate phase group is (μ4)^4/μ4, of size 64. Fixing the last diagonal coordinate to 1 gives representatives with exponents a,b,c in Z/4. The defining quartic is preserved term-by-term. The projective graph of each automorphism is an algebraic codimension-two cycle on S×S.

The action on the holomorphic two-form is i^(a+b+c) via the residue transformation law. The Fermat quartic K3 has Picard number 20 and transcendental lattice rank 2 with intersection Gram diag(8,8). On this rational transcendental subspace, the diagonal phase actions form the Q-algebra Q(i), represented by powers of the rotation matrix J=[[0,-1],[1,0]], whose rational span has dimension 2. **Caution:** this is a computation for restrictions to T(S), not a 22×22 action or a proof that their global graph-cycle classes span every rational Hodge class.

## First proof boundary
To certify the full Hodge class subspace of H⁴(S×S,Q) one must establish the rational algebraic divisor subspace and all correspondences acting on the transcendental part, including any off-diagonal decomposition. Our graph enumeration alone does not do so. STOP at missing full cycle-span comparison. This is not a failed Hodge conjecture.
