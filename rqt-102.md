## Mathematical scope and proof obligations
The Fermat quartic has NS rank 20 and transcendental lattice rank 2. For a rational K3-type rank-two transcendental Hodge structure with complex multiplication Q(i), End_Hdg(T)=Q(i), of rational dimension 2. The phases constructed in RQT-101 give I and J, which are Q-linearly independent. Orthogonal projectors to NS and T in H² are algebraic using rational inverse intersection matrices of divisor classes, diagonal correspondence and projectors for H⁰,H⁴. Thus the projected graph classes realize the 2 transcendental generators.

By Künneth and rational Hodge decomposition, cross terms NS⊗T and T⊗NS contribute no rational (2,2) classes, because T contains no rational (1,1) vectors. Hence dim Hdg⁴(S×S,Q)=2+20²+2=404, and the constructed cycles have the same independent span. This argument **proves the Hodge conjecture in degree four for this particular S×S**, as a rederivation of a known special case, not the conjecture in general.

**STOP** at general smooth projective X; Picard rank and CM symmetries cannot be assumed. The October 2026 claimed broad theorem for arbitrary K3 products was withdrawn and is not used here.
