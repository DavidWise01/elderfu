## Exact computation

RQT-130's W is an integral isometry of the Fermat quartic Néron–Severi lattice NS with induced finite discriminant action Q=[[7,4],[4,7]] mod 8 in the fixed RQT-114 gluing generators. The geometric transcendental lattice is T(S)=diag(8,8). All integral automorphisms of that rank-two positive-definite lattice are signed permutation matrices, precisely eight. Each is verified by J^T T J=T and compared modulo eight with Q. No J matches Q. Under the chosen gluing, an integral H² isometry extending W would need an integral T-isometry inducing the same finite discriminant action. None exists. Consequently THIS W **does not extend to an integral isometry of the full K3 H²**; it cannot be induced by a geometric K3 automorphism.

Independent extra exact check: the hyperplane class h obtained from four coplanar Fermat lines has h²=4, (Wh)²=4, and h·Wh=220. These numbers do not repair the gluing obstruction. The ample/Kähler-cone gate is not needed to reject this W; in particular no claim of ample preservation has been made.

This is not a contradiction to the existence of W in O(NS), to the even unimodular 22-class Gram construction, or to the known Torelli theorem. It illustrates why discriminant and period matching are essential. The exact test is [rqt-131.py](./rqt-131.py), run with RQT-130.py and RQT-114.py. It provides an exact, reproducible *negative* certificate.

The next natural boundary is to find an NS-isometry whose discriminant action lies in the image of O_Hdg(T) (for this rank-two Fermat period plane, the holomorphic-period-preserving subgroup is the four quarter-turn rotations), then check the ample chamber. Stop at any failed gate; the general Hodge conjecture remains OPEN.

Three independent witnesses A NS, B T, C gluing; no quorum. Preserve {{ i + h }}, {[(i)]}, .dlw, ENDER, cubit, full ASCII and immutable RQT-068.
