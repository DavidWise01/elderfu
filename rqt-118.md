## Source and exact computation
Let Δ=(1−g_x)(1−g_y)(1−g_z)C be the eight-patch closed cycle from RQT-117, with C the positive real Fermat simplex scaled by an eighth root of unity. This is a Pham vanishing cycle for the affine hypersurface x⁴+y⁴+z⁴=−1. The classical Pham formula, as presented in Movasati's *A Course in Hodge Theory*, chapter 12, equation (12.2), gives the following for n=2 and the distinguished vanishing cycles δ_a for multiindices a in {0,1,2}³:

- δ_a · δ_a = −2.
- If b−a has each entry 0 or 1, not all 0, the intersection is −(−1)^sum(b−a).
- Use symmetry for the reverse direction and 0 for other cases not covered by these comparisons.

Hence for δ_000=Δ and δ_100=g_x Δ:
```
       [ -2  +1 ]
  G =  [ +1  -2 ], det(G)=3, eigenvalues (-1,-3)
```
The nondegenerate negative-definite span is rank two and is **not** an integral realization of the known positive-definite transcendental lattice T(S)=diag(8,8).

From RQT-117's explicit holomorphic residue integral, ∫Δω=−√2 Γ(1/4)^3/(32 Γ(3/4)) ≈ −1.7187964545050932. Coordinate rotation g_x*ω=iω makes the second period i times the first. The two periods are independent over R; therefore the real projections of these cycles to the transcendental two-plane are independent. Nevertheless their actual integral classes contain algebraic components, since their rank-two intersection form is negative definite.
