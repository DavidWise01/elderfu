## Mathematical proof boundary
The known Nikulin symplectic involution ι on a K3 surface S acts on rational H²(S,Q) with +1 eigenspace dimension 14, and −1 eigenspace dimension 8. Define P±=(I±ι*)/2. The quotient-pullback/pushforward composition (with graph conventions fixed as in RQT-107) gives q=(I+ι*)v=2P+v. Store s=P−v **before** performing the quotient, then reconstruct v=q/2+s. All three operations are rational algebraic correspondences: diagonal, graph of ι, and their rational linear combinations. This is a reversible *augmented representation*, not an inverse to quotient data by themselves.

An actual exact-arithmetic local test evaluated 22 rational basis states plus 108 deterministic rational mixed states, 130/130 successful. Eight anti-invariant basis vectors map to zero under q, proving quotient-only non-injectivity. Script in [rqt-108.py](./rqt-108.py).

## Explicit STOP
The symbolically retained side channel is not a claim of physically reconstructing data absent from the quotient; it had to be stored from the original S. The test does not establish a full integral-lattice isomorphism or derive a universal algebraic-cycle witness for arbitrary Hodge classes. Hodge conjecture remains open. Three independent witness reports, no quorum, frozen RQT-068 unchanged.
