## Algebraic operator

On the Fermat quartic S:x⁴+y⁴+z⁴+w⁴=0, the map g([x:y:z:w])=[ix:y:z:w] is a genuine algebraic automorphism of order four. It preserves the defining equation and fixes the class of a hyperplane section. Its graph is an explicit algebraic correspondence on S×S. On w=1, the residue form omega=dx∧dy/(4z³) is multiplied by i.

## Exact integral audit

From RQT-114's exact 48-line intersection matrix G, selected 20-line Gram B and dual discriminant generators U, compute the permutation of all 48 lines under g, then the integral W in the actual line basis. Verify WᵀBW=B, W⁴=I, det W=−1, and W h=h with h²=4. The action on the discriminant group in the *fixed* RQT-114 generators is Q_g=[[0,7],[1,0]] modulo eight. It equals the integral action of J=[[0,−1],[1,0]] on T(S)=diag(8,8), as checked on all 64 elements of (Z/8)². The formally oriented period eigenvector (1,−i) transforms by +i. Note that choosing an orientation on an integral transcendental basis is distinct from explicitly identifying that basis with individual geometric closed cycles.

As g is a projective automorphism it preserves the Kähler cone; this is a direct geometric fact. The preserved very ample h is an additional exact check. Thus g supplies an unambiguous positive case of the global Torelli conditions. It does not repair the failed integral extension of the *different* operator W in RQT-131: that operator has the incompatible discriminant matrix [[7,4],[4,7]].
